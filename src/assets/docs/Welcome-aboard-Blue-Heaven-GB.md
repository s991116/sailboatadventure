# Welcome aboard Blue Heaven

On Blue Heaven we are a team. We do not expect you to know everything, but we do expect you to take part, ask if you are unsure, and speak up if something does not feel right.

# How we sail together

* **Safety first.** Weather, the boat and the crew always come before schedule and destination.  
* **We help each other.** Watches and practical tasks are shared according to ability and experience.  
* **We say things in time.** Fatigue, seasickness, worries and disagreements are easier to handle when we talk about them early.  
* **We look after our resources.** Water, power, gas, fuel and space are limited.  
* **We respect rest.** Sleep and quiet are part of safety, especially on longer passages.  
* **We keep things tidy.** Personal gear is stowed securely, and shared areas are left clear.

**Our most important shared rule:** If you are in doubt, ask. If you see something unsafe, say so at once.
