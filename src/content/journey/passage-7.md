If conditions and timing allow, a stop in Cape Verde can offer a cultural and practical midpoint before the final stretch west. From there — or directly from the Canaries — we follow the steady trade winds toward Barbados, where the turquoise waters of the Caribbean mark the beginning of a new chapter of our adventure.
