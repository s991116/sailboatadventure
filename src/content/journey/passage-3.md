Along the way, we hope to explore the wild landscapes of Islay and Jura, experience the dramatic scenery of Ireland's west coast and spend time on the Dingle Peninsula, where ancient history, rugged cliffs and Atlantic nature meet.

The journey ends at the Isles of Scilly, a special place for sailors preparing for longer passages. Here we will take time to complete preparations, watch the weather and get ready for the next chapter of our adventure: crossing the Atlantic towards the Azores.
